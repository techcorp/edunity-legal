Delete your Edunity account

Last updated: 13 November 2026

You can permanently delete your Edunity account and your data at any time. Deletion cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Edunity and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the Settings icon at the top right.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete account.

Your account is removed straight away and you are signed out.

Can't open the app?

Email support@aikafanda.com from the email address on your Edunity account, with the subject "Delete my Edunity account". We will confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and profile photo.
  • Your posts, comments and replies, including replies other students wrote under your comments.
  • Your likes, saved posts, follows and followers.
  • Your conversations and the messages in them, for both people.
  • Your blocks, mutes, reports you filed, notifications and push notification registrations.
  • Your sign-in details.

What may remain for a short time

  • Encrypted backups held by our hosting provider clear within a short period after deletion.
  • Our email provider may keep delivery logs of the verification and password-reset emails we sent you, under its own policy.

See the Privacy Policy for how we handle data, or write to support@aikafanda.com with any question.
