Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Edunity collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Edunity is operated by AikaFanda ("we", "us"). Contact: support@aikafanda.com.

In short

  • We collect what you give us to run a student network: your account, profile, posts, comments and messages.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, use advertising trackers, or access your location or contacts.
  • Your messages are only visible to the people in the conversation.
  • You can delete your account in the app and we remove your data.

1. Information we collect

WhatDetails
Account Email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash).
Profile Full name, username, bio, country, city, institution, program, semester, graduation year, the subjects you choose, and a profile photo if you add one.
Content and activity Posts, comments, likes, saved posts, who you follow and who follows you, and private text messages.
Safety Reports you send, and the students you block or mute.
Notifications An Expo push token, your device platform and an installation identifier, so we can send you notifications you have not turned off.
Technical Crash and error reports (error details, device model, operating system and app version, and an IP address as part of normal reporting). Short-lived counters of recent actions, kept for up to two days, that protect Edunity from spam.

If you choose a profile photo, Edunity accesses only the photo you select. We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, or your microphone or camera data.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, show your profile and content to other students, and deliver messages.
  • To send verification, password-reset and notification messages.
  • To keep Edunity safe: review reports, enforce our Terms, and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To find and fix bugs and keep the service reliable.

3. Who can see what

  • Other students can see your name, username, bio, photo, posts, comments, and follower counts. Your institution and semester are shown only if you leave those settings on (Settings, Privacy).
  • Messages are visible only to you and the person you are talking to. If either of you reports a message, our team can read it to review the report.
  • Blocked students cannot see your posts or contact you, and they are not told you blocked them.

4. Who we share it with

We use a few service providers to run Edunity. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase - hosts our database, sign-in and file storage (servers in Singapore).
  • Resend - delivers our verification and password-reset emails.
  • Expo - delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Sentry - receives crash and error reports.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the service.

5. How long we keep it

  • We keep your information while your account is active.
  • When you delete a post or comment it is hidden from everyone straight away. It may stay in our database in a hidden state until you delete your account.
  • When you delete a message you sent, it is removed for both people.
  • When you delete your account we permanently remove your profile, posts, comments, likes, saves, follows, blocks, mutes, reports you filed, push registrations and conversations, and we remove your profile photo. Backups kept by our hosting provider clear within a short period afterwards.
  • Reports about content are kept for safety review and are removed when that content or account is deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and choose what others see in Settings.
  • Mute or block students and report content from the "more options" menu.
  • Edit or delete your posts and comments, and delete your messages.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile, Settings, Delete account), or by email. See Delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or remove it, by emailing support@aikafanda.com. We reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local data-protection law, and you can exercise them the same way.

7. Where your data is processed

Our database and files are hosted in Singapore. Our service providers may process limited data in other countries. We use providers that protect data to a reasonable standard.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is protected by access rules in our database so that students can only reach what they are meant to. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Age

Edunity is for people aged 16 and over. If we learn that someone younger has an account, we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make a significant change we will tell you in the app or by email. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.
